01 / About

We build, own and support businesses intended to last.

Gammanite is an independent holding and operating company with interests across Asia. We combine long-term ownership, operating discipline and cultural intelligence to create enduring value.

02 / Markets

Asia, in relation.

Gammanite operates from Singapore across Taiwan, India and Japan. We see these markets not as isolated territories, but as a connected system of capability, capital, culture and demand.

01

Taiwan

Taiwan sits near the frontier of human technology. Its semiconductor and precision-manufacturing ecosystems help make modern artificial intelligence possible.

Beyond chips, Taiwan represents something equally valuable: engineering density, supplier trust and a culture of quiet industrial excellence.

02

India

India is entering a consequential decade. Economic growth, expanding infrastructure, urbanisation and a more discerning consumer are increasing the scale of the market while raising the standard expected of products and services.

We believe India’s next chapter will be defined not only by how much it produces and consumes, but by how well things are conceived, made and delivered.

03

Japan

Japan remains a reference point for design, manufacturing and continuous refinement.

Its relationships with India and Taiwan bring together capital, technology, infrastructure and industrial knowledge, creating capabilities intended to last.

04

Singapore

Singapore provides the legal, financial and institutional centre from which these markets can be connected.

India × Japan

Japanese capital is already participating in India’s growth through institutions such as MUFG, while Japanese financing and Shinkansen expertise support the Mumbai–Ahmedabad high-speed rail corridor.

03 / Industries

From the perishable
to the permanent.

We are interested in the systems and objects that shape everyday life: what people eat, drink, use, keep and pass on.

01

Food & Hospitality

Perishable and non-perishable foods, specialty coffee, culinary products, fine dining and contemporary mixology.

02

Home & Living

Furniture, tableware, storage, domestic tools and thoughtfully designed objects for everyday life.

03

Essential Instruments

Fundamental devices, tools and instruments whose intelligence may lie in their material, proportion and function rather than in a microchip.

04

Design & Manufacturing

Product development, material intelligence, regional manufacturing and cross-border distribution.

Across these areas, our interest is consistent: to bring greater consideration to how products are designed, how they are made and how they reach the people who use them.
